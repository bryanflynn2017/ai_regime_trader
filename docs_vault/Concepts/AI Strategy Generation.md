# AI Strategy Generation

AI strategy generation means proposing new strategies or variations from observed history and research ideas.

## Current Status
Post-1.0 priority.

## Role
AI-generated strategies should be treated as hypotheses, not truth.

Post-1.0, AI strategy generation can feed the [[Opportunity Discovery Engine]]
by proposing what kinds of movers, catalysts, and setup structures are worth
searching for outside the fixed watchlists.

## Validation Path
Every AI-generated idea should pass:
- [[Backtesting]]
- [[Walk-Forward Validation]]
- [[Real-World Validation]]
