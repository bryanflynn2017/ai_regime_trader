# Experiment Tracking

Experiment tracking records strategy ideas, settings, test windows, metrics, and conclusions.

## Current Status
Priority 3 post-1.0 goal.

## Role
Prevents [[Strategy Optimization]] and [[AI Strategy Generation]] from becoming guesswork.

## Required Links
Each experiment should connect:
- idea or hypothesis
- settings and thresholds
- [[Backtesting]] results
- [[Walk-Forward Validation]] results
- forward-test notes from [[Real-World Validation]]
- decision to adopt, revise, or reject
